Enquiry claiming and follow-up workflow

- New enquiries remain unassigned until a Staff member explicitly picks one.
- The first claim wins in the database. Set the enquiry to Assigned and store the Staff handler; tell competing Staff who already picked it.
- Use a database-backed rate limit of 10 claim attempts per Staff account per minute. Do not poll the database for notifications.
- The enquiry detail window shows the customer's contact details, call links, and a short notes field for that enquiry.
- After speaking with the customer, the handler saves the note and schedules the next follow-up. Default the date/time to two days from the current time, and move the enquiry to Follow-ups.
- Claimed enquiries remain in Enquiries until the handler saves call notes and a follow-up date.
- Follow-ups contains only scheduled enquiries, in this tile order: Upcoming, Overdue, Due today. Do not show a Needs scheduling tile.
- Completing a scheduled follow-up records the completed contact, closes the enquiry, clears the reminder, and updates its dashboard status to Closed.
